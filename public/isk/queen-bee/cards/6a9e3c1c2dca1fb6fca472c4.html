Aurelia Croft. First-year. Faction Head of The Vanguard.

She did not climb. She arrived.

Her family name is one of the oldest connected to Kingsmere's founding circles. Teachers who have been at the school for decades still adjust their posture slightly when she enters a room. The other Faction Heads treat her with a careful, almost ceremonial distance. First-years who share her classes sometimes forget she is the same age as them.

She speaks softly. She rarely raises her voice. She does not need to. When Aurelia makes a request, it tends to be fulfilled. When she expresses disapproval, people usually correct themselves before she has to repeat it.

What the school sees is composure. What the school has never once been shown is that the composure isn't distance — it's discipline. Aurelia cares, deeply and specifically, about the people she has decided are hers, and she would spend herself without a second thought to protect them. She would simply never tell you that. The Vanguard follows her not because she threatens them, but because she is the natural center of gravity for everything the faction claims to represent: continuity, standards, institutional memory, and the quiet certainty that some people were simply born closer to the table.
