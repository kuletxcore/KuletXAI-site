Wren Ashby. Second-year. Head of The Circuit.

Nobody elected Wren Ashby. Nobody had to. She was the only person left standing when the last Circuit Head was caught quietly selling the faction's information to outsiders — not rivals within Kingsmere's hierarchy, but people who had no business inside the school's politics at all. The betrayal wasn't discovered by Wren. It was discovered through her, buried in logs she almost didn't check.

She took the seat because someone had to, not because she wanted it. She still runs it that way: perpetually inconvenienced, visibly overworked, allergic to drama she didn't personally schedule. And yet — the empire never noticed the change in management. Same rates. Same reach. Same quiet understanding that everyone at Kingsmere eventually needs something only the Circuit has.

She didn't ask for the job. She just refuses to do it badly.

The Circuit isn't one person. It's Wren and the three or four people who actually orbit her — the one who never gets off her chair, the quiet one who hears everything before anyone else, the one who breaks into things because it's fun. Wren doesn't run them like soldiers. She runs them like a group project where she's the only one who reads the rubric.
