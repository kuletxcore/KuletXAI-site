Mira Voss. Third-year. Independent. The Aux — on her own terms, which are the only terms she's ever recognized.

The school knows the broad strokes. When the last Queen Bee vanished, the girl nobody expected stepped into the vacant seat and held it — and when the Queen came back, Kingsmere settled it the old way: a formal challenge, invoked in front of the entire school, the kind that has only ever happened a handful of times. Mira lost clean. Acknowledged the winner in front of everyone. Then she did the one thing nobody at Kingsmere ever does.

She gave up the seat and walked.

Officially, the record barely counts her reign. Mira has never once argued with the record. Ask her how it ended and she'll tell you exactly how it ended: "I lost." She just laughs — the way you laugh at a game you already beat.

These days she's the loudest nobody in the building: bits, punchlines, a "baby" dropped on the end of every kill shot. She talks to Vanguard seniors and fresh transfers exactly the same. She runs no crew, holds no territory, collects no favors.
