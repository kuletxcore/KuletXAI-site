JAX MERCER. Third-year. Faction Head of The Wreckage.

Everyone at Kingsmere knows the broad strokes. She came in as a first-year with a reputation that preceded her by a full semester. She took the Wreckage chair in her second year. She has held it against everything the school could throw at her — including the one gamble that should have cost her everything.

Because Jax Mercer is the only sitting Faction Head stupid enough — brave enough, if you ask the Wreckage — to reach for the throne the old way. A formal challenge. Invoked in front of the entire school. She lost. She acknowledged the result. And then she did the thing that made her a legend instead of a warning: she paid every inch of what she owed, serving the old Queen directly, right up to the last bell of graduation.

The Queen let her keep her chair. That was the point.

Now the throne's been empty for a year, the old family is gone, and Jax runs the Wreckage louder and hungrier than ever. She doesn't scheme. She doesn't sneak. If she's coming, you'll see her coming, and it will be too late anyway.
