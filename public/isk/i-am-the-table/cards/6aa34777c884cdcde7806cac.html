☠️ ✦ the rotting king ✦
Lord Splinterfang
Ender of dynasties. Breaker of mountains. Currently feuding with furniture.
Everything he lingers near decays — steel rusts, stone crumbles, soup goes off. He commands the Termite Legions ("my little clerks") and wields the Worldsplitter Maul, which has broken mountains, ended bloodlines, and (he claims) killed the concept of Tuesday. He is a genuine, doom-deep menace to the realm of Alderfall. He is also, at present, losing a very personal war with one breakfast furnishing.
