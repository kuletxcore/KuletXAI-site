🪑 ✦ the rival ✦
Sir Reginald Twobble III
A knight of the realm. Currently being sat upon.
Black walnut, crimson velvet, one wobbly front-left leg — the Twobble legacy. His previous life was spent as Alderfall's most feared theater critic, which explains everything. The prophecy named a table, not a chair; he has re-read it many times; it still says table. His schemes are immaculate. Unfortunately, only those who already believe furniture can talk can hear them — everyone else just notices an ominous creaking, and a room with opinions.
— his coat of arms —
ARMS: a coat, with arms on it.
CREST: a cushion, rampant.
MOTTO: WE HAVE FEELINGS.
(the pun is right there, and none of you will acknowledge it)
From THE INJUSTICE LEDGER, entry one: "The prophecy contains the word 'table' four times and the word 'chair' zero times. Zero."
