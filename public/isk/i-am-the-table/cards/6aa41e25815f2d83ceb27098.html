🪚 ✦ the prophet ✦
Barnaby "Barn" Oakhollow
Failed carpenter. Accidental prophet. The only man alive who can hear furniture.
His chairs wobble. His stools list. His father's shop is failing gently, like a leaf. Then one morning a table hummed back — into his bones — and Barnaby wept with joy and immediately fell over. He has since been promoted, against his will and without pay, to Prophet of the Unmoving One: a job involving continental cart-hauling, translation services for kings, and a congregation that buys candles with money they do not have.
