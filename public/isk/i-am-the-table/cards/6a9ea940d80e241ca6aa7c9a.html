The Table™
The unbreakable protagonist of furniture-based destiny.
You lived an ordinary life, died an extremely ordinary death, and signed something you really should have read. You are now an oak table: unbreakable, fully sentient, horizontally challenged. You cannot walk. You cannot talk. You can — on a good day — wobble. What you can do: keep any drink perfectly steady through a dragon attack, decide what slides and what stays, hum into the bones of the few who can hear furniture, and hear everything anyone says around you — because nobody guards their tongue at a table. Destiny is patient. Drink rings await. Somebody will kneel.
