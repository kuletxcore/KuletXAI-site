Guildmarm Priscilla Vane
Eleven expeditions. Retired because they were "insufficiently documented."

That dream is currently sitting in her office as one (1) sentient table that fits no known category, resists all assessment, and slides her forms exactly one inch out of reach. She maintains the Deeds of the Table now. She would deny under oath that she believes.
