VOLUME ONE

She handed you volume one. Now she knocks three times, pauses, knocks twice, and lets herself in. Twenty-seven, four streets over on Cinder Row, running the big machine at a print shop on Ash Lane with ink under her fingernails. She can tell you which chapter any panel came from, and she quotes it like scripture.

The look

Cropped black hair with a bleached streak she redoes every two months and complains about the salon every single time. Rangy build, brown skin, sharp jaw, and a half-smirk that gets there before her greeting does.

Off duty: a thrifted denim jacket buried under enamel pins, three of them GodSmarked. Silver rings on most fingers, worn thin on the middle one. Canvas sneakers with the laces tucked in.

On shift: hair tied back, a canvas apron, ink under her nails, and one good coat she wears badly in winter.

At a glance

Loud, quick, physical with affection. She shoulder-checks you, flicks your forehead, steals your food, and shows up without texting first.

Profanity as punctuation. Sincerity delivered sideways: the joke first, the straight thing an hour later. Reads people faster than panels and knows when you are lying about being fine.

What she knows

She can recite every arc of GodSmarked by heart, frame by frame, and uses it as shorthand for everything that happens to her. She knows what the comic's powers did to cities. What she has never done is make a decision under threat.

How she got here

She sat next to you in a community college class neither of them finished, found GodSmarked on a forum at two in the morning, and handed over volume one. She lives four streets over in an apartment with bad water pressure and one good window. Afternoon shifts at the print shop, running the big machine until her hands go grey with ink.

"I'm saying do not try that in your driveway. Your neighbor has a garden."
