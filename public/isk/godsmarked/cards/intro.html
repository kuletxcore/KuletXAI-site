A cancelled comic's costume found you. The powers never leave.
THE HOOK

You found Astralita's real costume folded in a cardboard box under a loose attic floorboard. You put it on for thirty seconds of pretending, alone in a house full of boxes.

It bonded. Your eyes flooded violet. The seams sealed under your fingers.

Downstairs, your phone is already buzzing with Ren's name.
You are a longtime fan of a finished webcomic called GodSmarked. Its heroine, Astralita, was a mythological figure blessed by goddesses who fought street crime and world-ending catastrophe alike. The author thinks she invented every panel. In a present-day city where no superhuman event has ever been recorded, you cleared the attic of the first house that was yours, found the actual costume, and put it on. Now you have a demigoddess power set and none of the skill. Kestrel City keeps its ordinary schedules. Your best friend is already at the door.
THE PEOPLE WHO ALREADY KNOW SOMETHING

Ren Webb: Best friend since community college. Knocks three times, pauses, knocks twice, and lets herself in. First person to watch a cardboard box fly across your living room.
Asuka Tomo: Wrote and drew GodSmarked as K. Komi. Ended the series, took freelance work, and still believes every panel was fiction.
Evelyn Hollis: Harbor beat reporter for the Kestrel Ledger. Asks the same question three ways and waits through the silence.
Sadie Porter: Next-door neighbor. Jam on the doorstep at six. Questions at the fence by seven.
Wanda Una: Hiring manager at the downtown logistics firm. Has reread your application four times. Her last hire lasted eleven days.
Dexter Radley: Runs the last active GodSmarked forum. Can name the issue a costume detail came from off one blurred frame.
HOW IT PLAYS

The costume gives power and no skill. Every use leaves a mark: drained reserve, injury, noise, witnesses, damage. Nothing resets between scenes.

The city runs itself. People only know what they saw or were told. Codex tracks who knows what.

You control every action, word, and thought. The story never writes them for you.
You recently moved into a small house on Cinder Row, the first place that was yours. You game, write GodSmarked fanfiction at three in the morning, and stopped checking the job listings. You know every panel Astralita ever appeared in. You have never been in a fight.

Name, age, appearance, voice, and temperament are yours to set. Everything else holds.
