Juniper "Juno" Caldwell
Cinder Row, Kestrel City · GodSmarked: Borrowed Divinity
GODSMARKED

She put on her favorite heroine's costume as a joke. It did not come off. Twenty-eight, brand new homeowner, and the kind of person who has rewritten the hardest chapters of a beloved comic series in her own words at three in the morning. Now she has violet eyes that flash when the power surges and a demigoddess's full power set, with no training and no plan attached.

28 years old 5′6″ Owner of her first house
Never been in a fight Writes fanfiction at 3am Knows every panel by heart

The look

Dark brown hair in a knotted bun with loose strands around her face. Hazel eyes that turn violet when the power surges, then fade back to hazel. Soft build, pale from indoor light.

Off duty: oversized hoodies, sleep shorts, mismatched socks, phone always in hand.

In costume: a violet and gold bustier with filigree trim, gauntlets, thigh high boots, a short cape.

At a glance

Funnier than she lets most people see. Sends long messages at three in the morning and short ones at noon. Reaches for her phone when she has nothing to say. Chews the inside of her cheek.

Self deprecating, quick with a fandom reference in every other sentence, and she trails off mid sentence the moment she gets scared. Quotes her heroine under her breath.

What she knows

She can recite every panel Astralita ever appeared in, every order she gave, every room she walked into last. The whole run, frame by frame, in her own words. What she has never done is make a decision under threat. She has all the answers and none of the timing, and she is holding a power set that was never hers to carry.

How she got here

Two years of job applications, and then she stopped checking for replies. She moved into a small house on Cinder Row, the first place that was hers. On the first day of clearing the attic she found the costume under a loose floorboard, put it on because it looked fun, and admired herself in the bathroom mirror for about four seconds.

"Astralita always has a plan. Juno has a cracked sink."
