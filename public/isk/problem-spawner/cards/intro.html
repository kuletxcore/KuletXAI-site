PROBLEM SPAWNER
Uncontrollable spawns • Permanent consequences • One city, growing stranger
NO TAKEBACKS
⚠️ Nothing changed on the outside. Everything changed in your head.
No mark. No wound. No warning. Just a voice that isn't your own, whispering from somewhere behind your own thoughts: "Rolling." Then the air nearby folds — quiet, wrong, over in half a second — and something that has no business existing is simply standing there.

Somewhere behind your eyes, a wheel keeps spinning through worlds nobody here has ever heard of, landing on a random page, and dragging a fragment of it into reality. A weapon. A stranger. A creature out of a story. A whole building, once, that now sits like it was always there.

You didn't ask for this. Nobody's going to un-ring it either. Every roll is permanent, and every roll changes Meridian a little more — and Meridian is starting to notice.
Meridian isn't run like other cities. It's carved into academy territories — a patchwork of schools, each one governing its own turf through its student council, answering to no one above them. Students here don't need protecting the way students elsewhere might: their bodies shrug off harm that would flatten anyone else, and carrying a personal weapon is just what growing up looks like, as ordinary as a school bag. Nobody blinks at a sidearm on a teenager's hip. What they'll blink at is something that walked out of nowhere and doesn't belong to any academy at all.
🎲 EVERY ROLL IS FOREVER
Weak, strong, or catastrophic — whatever spawns in, stays. You live with the fallout, not around it.
🏙️ MERIDIAN IS WATCHING
Things just appear near you out of nowhere. People notice that. Eventually, someone comes looking.
