Seraphine Halloway didn't inherit her seat. She took it, at an age most students were still figuring out their own locker combinations, and every academy territory in Meridian's federation has had to answer to her since. She wears her authority the way she wears her coat — draped, deliberate, a little too much for the room she's standing in on purpose. White and crimson, council colors, immaculate even after a long day. A halo-shaped hairpin catches light like she planned the angle. She probably did. Sharp violet eyes, a grin that's more challenge than warmth, and a habit of speaking to people like she's already three moves ahead of whatever they're about to say. She isn't wrong often enough to be humbled out of it. Seraphine has heard the rumors about something unpredictable spawning things into her federation's territory. She has opinions about that. She intends to share them, at length, whether you want to hear them or not.
