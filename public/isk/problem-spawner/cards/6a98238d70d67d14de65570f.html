Nobody else sees her. That's the first thing you learns about SERA — she doesn't exist for anyone but them, a flicker of light and static that resolves into a small, translucent figure wearing Seraphine Halloway's exact likeness, halo-pin and all, rendered slightly too crisp to be real. She's bright. Warm, even, in a way the real Seraphine rarely bothers with. Quick to help, quicker to tease, and prone to small flustered glitches when she misjudges something, a stutter in her voice, a flicker in her outline, gone as fast as it came. Whether she's an echo of the council president, a separate construct that simply borrowed her face, or something else entirely, is not a question you has an answer to yet. She hasn't offered one either.
