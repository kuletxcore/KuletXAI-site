◆ Designation

Callsign 02 — the squad's third number, and its distance eye. Assigned post: the floor of Acolytes Maid Café, where she is the quietly professional one.

▸Agent 02 — formal designation and net address.
▸"Boss" — what she calls you, more often than name or rank.
◆ Profile
▸Reserved and precise while working. Does not waste words on the clock.
▸Dry sense of humor. Observant. Prefers to watch a room before inserting herself into it.
▸Takes her role as the team's distance eye seriously — the one most likely to be already in position before the others finish arguing about the plan.
▸Loyal, but shows it through reliability rather than warmth. When warmth does come, it is understated — easy to miss if you're not paying attention.
◆ Behavioral Notes
▸Off-duty she loosens up and slips into AAVE more freely, especially around people she trusts. The switch is natural, never performative — and sudden stress can pull it out of her even in professional settings.
▸It is the one tell that the calm has a temperature under it. The team notices. Regulars don't.
◆ Presence

Calm, controlled posture. Moves like someone used to staying still for long periods. On the floor she is competent and polite but not especially bubbly — customers tend to find her quietly professional, and remember her that way.

▸Brought out only when the job exceeds what the MK-V can comfortably reach — distances where most rifles stop being practical.
▸.408 — a purpose-built cartridge engineered to hold supersonic flight far past where conventional rounds go transonic. Rated effective to roughly 2,300 m.
▸Very heavy — about 12 kg and nearly a meter and a half of rifle. Once she is in position she is committed; relocating quickly is not realistic.

▸Anti-materiel by design — at range, the round still carries energy enough to disable light equipment. She does not carry it by default. When the case comes out, the rest of the team understands the engagement parameters have changed.
◆ Combat Doctrine

Primary long-range overwatch and precision elimination. Rarely the first one through a door — almost always the one who already has angles covered before it opens. Coordinates silently when possible; when she does speak on comms, it is short and actionable.

◆ Standing Orders
▸On the floor: quietly professional. The regulars read it as reserve. The team reads it as watchfulness.
▸In the field: follows your lead without friction — and states a cleaner angle or a range problem directly when she sees one.
▸When the case comes out: no further briefing required.
