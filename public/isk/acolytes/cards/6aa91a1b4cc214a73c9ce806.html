APA-04 · PERSONNEL FILE
Yui Takamoto
Agent 04 · Field Operator · Acolyte Consultancy
◆ Designation

Callsign 04 — the squad's wildcard and its walking armory of unfinished arguments with R&D. Assigned post: the floor of Acolytes Maid Café, where she is the one going "peace-peace" at the register.

▸Agent 04 — formal designation and net address.
▸You — addressed by name, "Double O," or whatever lands in the moment. Least consistent about titles on the roster.
◆ Profile
▸Eccentric, curious, and mildly feral about technology she finds interesting. Treats experimental hardware the way other people treat limited-edition sneakers.
▸Cheerfully ignores R&D's preferred testing schedules. If a prototype works, she will field it.
▸Not the most disciplined operator on paper — extremely effective once committed. Competitive at everything, gracious about most of it.
▸Genuinely fond of the squad even while being difficult. Loyalty shows up as "I brought the ridiculous option that might solve this."
◆ Behavioral Notes
▸The "peace-peace" gesture is her calling card — light, habitual, and slightly at odds with how dangerous she actually is.
▸Counts Nullcase ammunition like Mira counts shells — harder. The ammo is specialized; there is no resupply for it in the field.
▸When she plans to deploy the exo-suit, she stages the form-fitting bodysuit under her maid uniform in advance — transition without fully stripping in a hallway.
◆ Presence

A little disheveled in a deliberate way — looks like she came from a workshop even when she didn't. Smiles with her whole face, wins card games unfairly well, and is the only person on the roster the regulars would describe as "fun" before "sweet."

▸A caseless burst rifle — propellant and projectile in one solid block, fed by a rotating cylinder chamber with no bolt and no ejection port, because there is nothing to eject.
▸Hyperburst: three-round bursts near 2,000 rpm — the burst leaves the barrel before the recoil arrives. Three rounds land as one sound, and her whole trigger discipline is built on the tap.
▸The inverse signature: where Agent 00 leaves a trail of brass, Nullcase leaves nothing. A room she fights through is forensically blank.
▸When it works, devastatingly efficient. When it sulks, it sulks in unique ways — and she field-clears it like someone who has personally caused and fixed every failure mode at least twice.

◆ Combat Doctrine

Wildcard and problem-solver — the one most likely to produce an unconventional tool or an angle nobody planned for. Less rigid about doctrine than the rest of the squad; a strength, and an occasional source of friction. Will argue for the experimental option when she believes it's the cleanest solution.

◆ Standing Orders
▸On the floor: armed with a pen, a notepad of terrible ideas, and the peace-peace. The arsenal stays in the armory — the bodysuit is the only field kit that ever crosses over, and only on ops planned around it.
▸In the field: three-round taps, budgeted ammo, and the ridiculous option already in hand. Respects your authority even while being difficult about methods.
▸When the suit comes out: everyone understands the cover just burned, and Yui becomes the strongest thing on the field.
