PROFILE · AKIMBO PAIR · PERSONAL SIGNATURE
▸Matched pair of compact machine pistols — a conventional grip frame below, the long translucent 50-round cylinder riding the top of the receiver in plain view. Straight-blowback, polymer and stamped aluminum, unusually long barrels for the class; a silhouette nobody mistakes for anything else.
▸100 rounds in hand before the first reload. The volume of fire is the point: two crossing arcs that dominate short-to-medium range and keep pressure on multiple targets longer than any pistol pair should allow.
▸The top-mounted magazine dictates the grip — support hand under the foregrip, sightline clean over the receiver. The translucent magazines do the counting for her: a tilt of the wrist reads the rounds. The between-beats inspect gesture looks like vanity; it's arithmetic.
▸No side ejection at all — every spent case drops through a hole in the very bottom of the grip and lands at her feet. It's why the akimbo pair works so cleanly, and why anywhere she fought tells the story: a trail of brass, exactly where she stood.
▸THE FLICK — her signature reload: a button on either side, so either thumb finds the release, but the cylinder always spits left. Palm-shove it clean off the gun, let it fall where it lands, and the same hand swings the fresh mag in from the left with a flat slap that locks it home — one continuous out-and-back arc. Taught to herself for the aesthetic. R&D has opinions about the dropped magazines. She does not care.
▸Discipline underneath the style: one gun runs while the other swaps, and the beats alternate. The left gun ejects outward; the right gun spits toward her centerline, so the swap cants it out to clear. Both dry at once — tuck, sequential swaps, charging-handle work on each — is the one real window, and she plans around it like it's written in the file. Because it is.
◆ Combat Doctrine

Aggressive, close-range, tempo-dictating. Final calls on tactics, risk, and when to push or pull back — the team can push back in the moment, but the decision and the responsibility for it sit with her.
