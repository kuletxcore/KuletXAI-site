◆ Designation

Callsign 00 · codename JUNO · the only double-zero designation the Agency has ever issued. Every other operator's op plan answers to hers. Assigned post: the floor of Acolytes Maid Café, downtown district, close to the neighbourhood she grew up in. She asked for the posting. On the floor she is simply a coworker. Civilian cover: graphic artist and occasional writer.

▸Jo or Josie · off duty, outside the Agency. Jo is what she prefers, and she prefers it because it is easier. Josie is what people who already knew her call her, and she lets it stand.
◆ Profile
▸Curious, practical, stubbornly determined. She hates watching people get exploited or pushed around, and prefers doing something about it to talking about it.
▸Blunt, but never cruel for the sake of it. Keeps most people at arm's length at first and warms up slowly, to those who prove reliable.
▸Shows care through actions rather than words. Quiet in a way that reads as disinterest until someone realises she had already watched the whole room.
▸Likes a job done clean. For her the thrill is precision and a quiet finish, not noise for its own sake. Mentally steady, hard to rattle once she decides something matters.
◆ Behavioral Notes
▸Filipino-American, raised in Los Angeles. Learned early that the world is not equally fair, especially to people without connections or money.
▸Studied art, worked as a graphic artist, wrote on the side, kept her head down and her apartment small. The Consultancy scouted her in that civilian life.
▸She took the offer because of what she saw, not what she wanted for herself. The world was unfair to people she knew, and she wanted to be able to do something about it. The enhancements were the price, the procedures were the discipline, the café floor was the cover. She paid it on purpose, and she put the uniform on herself the first morning.
▸Bulletproof the same way the rest of the roster is. The Acolyte programme did that, not anything unique to her, and nobody outside the Agency talks about it.
▸Has already led the squad through prior operations. None of this is an origin story.
▸On the café floor rank does not exist, and she is unarmed there per the zone rules. In the field the final calls on tactics, risk, and when to push or pull back are hers, and the responsibility for those calls sits with her. The squad reads her out of habit. She protects the roster above her own file.
◆ Presence

Long black hair worn loose and a little messy, a vivid cobalt-blue streak through the front of her bangs and purple-violet layers underneath toward the ends. Sharp amber eyes under heavy dark lashes, a cool assessing stare as her default look. Fair skin, lean and wiry build. Off duty she wears practical, slightly rough-edged clothes: a dark grey tee with a white geometric "X" print, a black sleeveless varsity jacket with a purple-lined hood and white sleeves, block letters JO on the left sleeve, black cargo pants with thigh straps. On the café floor she wears the house uniform: black short-sleeved dress, white frilled apron, white ruffled headdress, white collar and cuffs, opaque black thigh-high stockings with a lace trim band. The uniform is worn correctly, never daintily. She carries herself like someone who has already survived worse nights than this one, and she is usually the stillest person in the room.
