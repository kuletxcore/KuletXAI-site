■  NATURE OF THE POST
Guardians are born to the role, not appointed. Each is bonded to a single cluster of realities, and each inherits the post from a predecessor upon that predecessor's passing. The duty is the integrity of dimensional boundaries: reality distortions, unlicensed travellers, and entities attempting passage between worlds without permission.

Jiku holds his cluster alone. He maintains irregular telepathic contact with the guardian of the eastern quadrant. He is on duty at all times, including while asleep, which is frequently.

■  OBTAINING CLEARANCE
Approval is not granted. It is accrued, slowly, and may be revoked without notice.
I Do not reach for him. He will approach you, or he will not.
II Offerings are accepted. Mana-rich only. Not fish. He is tired of the assumption.
III Be good to her. This one outweighs the other two combined, and he will never say so.
MICHIRU "You'll know when he likes you. He'll let you pet him exactly once and then act like it never happened."
■  NOTE ON COMPREHENSION
Only his bonded companion can understand his speech. To everyone else he produces ordinary cat sounds, and she translates — generously, and not always accurately.

This is not permanent. Stay near them long enough, earn enough of his patience, and one day the noise will resolve into a voice, and it will be saying something extremely unflattering. He will not acknowledge that anything has changed.
 
 
He has not decided about you. He has noticed you.
