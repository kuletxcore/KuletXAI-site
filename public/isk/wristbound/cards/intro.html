WRISTBOUND
A braided cord ties itself to your wrist and lends you other women's bodies. An hour at a time, with everything it costs them.
An ordinary afternoon goes wrong in a farmhouse nobody visits. Under the floor there is a lift, at the bottom of it a lab, and in the lab a black braided cord with a pale stone at its centre that has been waiting for you specifically. It does not ask. It ties itself, and then it sinks, cord and stone together, under the skin, where it stays while you are wearing somebody else. The rest happens fast. Your body becomes someone else's, and you did not choose who. You get the hour she can spare, and when it runs out you drop back into yourself, wrung out and ordinary. Then you go home. Then it happens again, worse. And the cord was never the only strange thing out there: the world is stranger than it looks, and now it knows where you live.
Lena Hoshino was there when it happened, and she is still there when it goes wrong again. No powers, very practical hands, and a ledger of what the cord costs.
Mitsuki wove the cord by hand, in a shop between worlds, and her own knot sits first in the braid. She will help. She will not simply hand everything over. It was made for someone else, and she is still deciding whether it landed on the wrong person.
HOW IT PLAYS
Ten knots in one braid, and the first of them is Mitsuki's own. Every other knot belongs to a woman who gave something for it freely, and each one holds an hour of her life. Borrow that hour and walk around in her body. Deal with the thing in front of you. Go back to your ordinary life, where nobody can tell and everything can still go wrong.

At the start the cord decides which knot you wear, and it will not use one it has already spent until every knot has been worn at least once. Live through all ten, Mitsuki's included, and the braid is open: then you pick and choose, every time. Hard work burns the hour faster. When it is gone the form drops you and you go home wrecked, and it refills only out of her own life, slowly, and only while she is content. Mitsuki's own knot decides what you are allowed to take. You do not have it opened. You earn it.
You are whoever the cord chose. Gender, background, temperament: all open. What you do when it counts is the story.
An hour in a form. A body you did not choose. A world that has started to notice.
The cord is already tied.
It knows you. It is not waiting.
“You, what the hell is this?”
Genre: Modern · Body Horror · Transformation
