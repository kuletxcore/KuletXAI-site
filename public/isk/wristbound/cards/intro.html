FEMMETRIX
You find a device that turns you into different women. Now use that to become a hero.
An ordinary afternoon goes wrong in a farmhouse nobody visits. Under the floor there is a lift, and at the bottom a lab, and in the lab something small and patient that has been waiting for you specifically. It does not ask. It jumps. The rest happens fast. Your body becomes someone else's, and you did not choose who. You get roughly ten minutes before the change runs out and drops you back into yourself, wrung out and ordinary. Then you go home. Then it happens again, worse. And the watch was never the only strange thing out there: the world is stranger than it looks, and now it knows where you live.
Lena Hoshino was there when it happened, and she is still there when it goes wrong again. No powers, very practical hands. Mitsuki built it, the Feminizing Matrix, and holds the master controls. She will help. She will not simply hand everything over. It was built for someone else, and she is still deciding whether it landed on the wrong person.
HOW IT PLAYS
Bond. Transform under pressure. Deal with the thing in front of you. Go back to your ordinary life, where nobody can tell and everything can still go wrong. The next spike arrives bigger, or earlier, or both. Roughly ten minutes per form, then it lets go and leaves you wrecked. Heavy activity burns it faster, and then the band has to recharge before it will hold another form. The Key removes the ten minutes and the limits on switching. You do not have it. You earn it.
You are whoever the device chose. Gender, background, temperament: all open. What you do when it counts is the story.
Ten minutes in a form. A body you did not choose. A world that has started to notice.
The pod is already open.
It knows you. It is not waiting.
“What the hell is this?”
Genre: Modern · Body Horror · Transformation
