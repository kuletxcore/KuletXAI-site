214 people were saturated by the discharge pulse. 213 died of cascade resonance — their bodies began to become something their skeletons could not carry. The subject was mid-rescue at the moment of saturation, holding a drowning woman at the surface. The woman did not survive.

Unauthorized shift, first on record — ~40 m, unprompted, to catch the falling Harbor expressway span over an evacuation column of four thousand. STRATEGIC RELEASE

Authorized four times. Executed three — all Cat-4 engagements. The fourth authorization was declined by the subject: the corridor was not clear. The refusal is in this file. So is what it prevented.

Contact Log — Extract

Deep contact (desig. HADAL). Est. 380 m at first surfacing; treat as lower bound — it moves through the bay as the bay. Three surfacing events on record. Pursues kaiju signatures; ignores humans and infrastructure unless obstructed. After kills, remains over the carcass for hours; the rifts go quiet for weeks afterward. Has never attacked the asset first. Orients on her shift signature, holds, does not commit. Deep-contact events are not classed as engagements. Classification: unresolved.

Dual-signature nights. Two incidents on record in which Colossus Actual's rift signature was logged in two places at once — on nights the subject was verifiably elsewhere. Ledger entries stand pending resolution. Instrumentation error has been ruled out twice.

Pre-shift behavior, invariant across three years: relocates on foot to clear ground before growth. Corridor casualties attributable to the asset: zero.

Post-engagement: 6–14 hours at reduced baseline function. Refuses assistance. Understatement under pressure is consistent and apparently involuntary.
