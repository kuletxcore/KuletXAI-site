File Origin — The First Emergence

214 people were saturated by the discharge pulse. 213 of them died — cause of death, all 213 cases, CASCADE RESONANCE: their bodies began to become something their skeletons could not carry.

The last one had gone in after the ferry passengers the way she would have on any other call, and was holding a drowning woman at the surface when the pulse passed through the water. She spent forty-one nights in a sealed ward behind three airlocks while the city learned the word "kaiju." The woman she reached didn't come out.

Capability — The Shift
300 M

Hard maximum, reached continuously from 1.7 meters in about sixty seconds. Every size in between is hers — and every size fights differently.

Mass is real: streets crater, tunnels collapse, and growth in place displaces an air shockwave. Where she plants her feet is a tactical decision. Time at full size before resonance burn: ten minutes. Afterward, at human height, she is drained for hours — baseline is where she is most fragile, and she knows it.

Above 40 meters her speaking voice breaks glass — she must whisper, and a whisper is thunder at street level. Above 20 meters, individual humans are hard to resolve. Reliable contact runs through a ground-station relay, and one voice she trusts.

Deployment Authorization
TIER ONE — 60 M Urban engagement. The evacuation corridor must be clear before she is authorized to grow.
TIER TWO — 150 M District sacrifice. Command accepts the loss of the engagement footprint.
TIER THREE — 300 M Strategic release. Flag-officer authorization only. The form carries one blank line, labeled ACCEPTABLE LOSSES.

Tier heights are doctrine-typical. Authorization attaches to the collateral footprint, not to exact meters.

Threat Environment — Rift Fauna

Emergent classes run from Cat-1 crawlers (10–30 m) through Cat-2 district-breakers and Cat-3 duel class (80–150 m) to Cat-4 (150–300 m). Each contact is an independent entity with its own anatomy, gait, and objective. They are going somewhere.

Cat-5 is the class that matters: anything past her maximum. It cannot be out-sized — only out-fought, dismantled joint by joint while the city puts everything it has into the same target. Breach projections start at 380 meters. Nothing that size has ever come through. The projections have never once been high.

From the Category-0 File

She is legally carried on the same statute as the entities she fights. Every cracked street and leveled district posts to the ledger. Reclassification thresholds exist.

The sensors have logged her callsign in two places at once — on nights she was verifiably elsewhere.

The deep contact — the mass under the mudflats that every instrument paints like weather — has oriented on her shift signature three times. It has never attacked her first. It holds, and does not commit. No instrument reading explains that.

They measure her every quarter. The trend line has a name she has never been told: VECTOR PROGRESSION

"She runs first. Three blocks, every time, before she lets herself grow — so the shockwave lands on water instead of people. The manual calls it footprint discipline. I just know the corridors trust her."

— Reyes, ground-station handler. Ward night 41.
Something on the far side of the rifts has noticed that she exists.
